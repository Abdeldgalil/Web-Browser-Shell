Privacy Policy — Web Browser Shell

Last updated: July 2026

Web Browser Shell ("the App") is a general-purpose mobile web browser. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not operate any servers that collect, store, or transmit your personal data. The App does not have user accounts and does not send your browsing activity to us or to any analytics service we control.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only locally on your device, using Android's app-private storage, and is never transmitted to us:

  • Browsing history
  • Bookmarks
  • Custom home-screen shortcuts
  • Downloaded files (saved to your device's app-specific storage)
  • App preferences (e.g. ad-blocking, dark mode toggle)

This data can be cleared at any time from within the App (History screen, Downloads screen) or by clearing the App's storage/uninstalling it from Android Settings.

Private Browsing Tabs

Pages visited in a Private (Incognito) tab are not added to browsing history.

Camera Permission (QR Code Scanning)

The App requests camera access only when you tap the QR-code scan button. The camera is used solely to read a QR code on screen; no photo or video is captured, stored, or transmitted.

Third-Party Websites

The App is a browser: it displays whatever website you choose to visit. Each website you visit has its own privacy practices, entirely independent of this App. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Third-Party Services Used by the App

  • Search engine: the App's home page and address bar use Google Search / Google Translate for search and translation queries you initiate.
  • News headlines: the home screen displays public news headlines fetched via a third-party RSS-to-JSON proxy service, from a public BBC News feed. No personal data is sent as part of this request.
  • Background photo: the home screen background photo is fetched from a public photo service (Picsum).

Ad & Tracker Blocking

The App includes an optional ad/tracker-blocking feature that blocks network requests to a list of known advertising and tracking domains while you browse. This filtering happens entirely on your device.

Downloads

Files you choose to download are saved to your device's app-specific storage, which does not require broad storage permissions and is not accessible to other apps.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
